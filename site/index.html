WebDAV setup guide

Your library stays on your device

Books and progress are stored locally. Export a library backup before clearing browser data or moving to another browser.

Sync is optional and requires your own WebDAV server. For browser sync, the server must allow cross-origin requests or be available through a same-origin proxy.
